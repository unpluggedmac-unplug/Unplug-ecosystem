Ad Banners

Each advertising slot on the site starts as a "Reserve this space" placeholder. Add one or more banners to a slot — with more than one, they rotate automatically on the live site (fade, with prev/next and dots). Recommended banner size: 1920 × 600px — landscape, 16:5. Accepted: JPG, PNG, WEBP; max 5 MB. Keep important text/logos away from the extreme edges (banners are responsive and may crop on small screens). Optionally add a narrower mobile image for phones. Untick "Active" or set a schedule window (start/end dates) to hold a banner back without deleting it — expired banners drop out of rotation automatically.

Competitions

Add a competition, or change an existing one's name, dates, entry fee and status. Open shows it on the site; Draft and Closed hide it while keeping every entry and vote. Changing the entry fee only affects new entries — anyone who has already entered keeps the fee they were charged. A competition's web address can't be changed, because the site's pages look it up by that name.
