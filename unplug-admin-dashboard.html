Ad Banners

Each advertising slot on the site starts as a "Reserve this space" placeholder. Add one or more banners to a slot — with more than one, they rotate automatically on the live site (fade, with prev/next and dots). Recommended banner size: 1920 × 600px — landscape, 16:5. Accepted: JPG, PNG, WEBP; max 5 MB. Keep important text/logos away from the extreme edges (banners are responsive and may crop on small screens). Optionally add a narrower mobile image for phones. Untick "Active" or set a schedule window (start/end dates) to hold a banner back without deleting it — expired banners drop out of rotation automatically.

Member Status

The member status ladder (Explorer through Hall of Fame). Members are auto-promoted based on their Unplug Score, days since joining, and qualified active months. Hall of Fame is admin-granted only — award it from the Member Scores section.

Streak Tiers

Members build a streak by completing at least one daily/weekly mission each day. Crossing a tier's day count pays a one-time bonus and resets when the streak breaks. Each tier needs a distinct day count.

Trust & Anti-Cheat

Members are auto-flagged for velocity (an abnormal burst of point-earning actions) or reciprocal recognition farming (a pair exchanging recognitions to inflate both scores). A flag docks trust but never bans outright — it only blocks recognising, being recognised, and Top 10 voting once trust drops below 50. Reset clears a false positive back to a clean 100.

Match an EFT against your bank statement by its reference code, then Approve. Approving emails the customer their download link and reference — that email is only ever sent after you approve. Nothing here is deleted, so rejected and used purchases stay on record.

Competitions

Add a competition, or change an existing one's name, dates, entry fee and status. Open shows it on the site; Draft and Closed hide it while keeping every entry and vote. Changing the entry fee only affects new entries — anyone who has already entered keeps the fee they were charged. A competition's web address can't be changed, because the site's pages look it up by that name.

The Top 10 is not listed here. It's a separate part of the site with its own page — manage its entries under Publish.
