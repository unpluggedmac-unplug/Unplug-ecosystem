Page Visibility

Take a page out of the menus without taking it down. An unlisted page still works for anybody you send the link to, and search engines are still told about it — it just stops appearing in the navigation and the footer.

This is not privacy. An unlisted page is completely public to anyone with the address, and it stays in the sitemap. It is for a page you hand out — a campaign link, a form in a social bio — rather than one people browse into. Anything that genuinely needs protecting needs an account, not this.

Member Status

The member status ladder (Explorer through Hall of Fame). Members are auto-promoted based on their Unplug Score, days since joining, and qualified active months. Hall of Fame is admin-granted only — award it from the Member Scores section.

Streak Tiers

Members build a streak by completing at least one daily/weekly mission each day. Crossing a tier's day count pays a one-time bonus and resets when the streak breaks. Each tier needs a distinct day count.

Trust & Anti-Cheat

Members are auto-flagged for velocity (an abnormal burst of point-earning actions) or reciprocal recognition farming (a pair exchanging recognitions to inflate both scores). A flag docks trust but never bans outright — it only blocks recognising, being recognised, and Top 10 voting once trust drops below 50. Reset clears a false positive back to a clean 100.

Top 10 Monthly Rankings

The record of how every month finished — #1 down to the last spot, with the votes each contestant had when the month closed. This is captured on its own shortly after each month ends, and the winner, runner-up and third place are given their badges at the same time.

Votes start again at zero each month, but nobody loses their place: the new month opens in the order the last one closed, and the board moves as soon as people start voting again. No votes are ever deleted — every bulk purchase stays on record against its payment, it simply belongs to the month it was bought in.
