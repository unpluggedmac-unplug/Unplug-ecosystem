# Growth Application — Deep Discovery questionnaire

Source status: **verbatim site-owner source supplied 2026-09-11**.

This document is the canonical recovered Deep Discovery questionnaire. Do not shorten, paraphrase, merge, reorder or invent questions without an explicit documented product decision.

## For individuals

### Identity

- What is your name and surname?
- What name would you like the public to know you by?
- Where are you based?
- What industry or industries are you interested in?
- How would you describe yourself in one sentence?
- What do you currently do?
- Are you studying, employed, self-employed, freelancing, looking for work, or exploring opportunities?
- What languages do you speak?
- Which languages are you comfortable working in?
- What makes you different from other people doing similar work?

### Direction

- What do you ultimately want to become?
- Where would you like to be professionally within the next 12 months?
- Where would you like to be in five years?
- What would success look like to you?
- What is the biggest dream you are currently working toward?
- Is there something you have always wanted to try but haven't had the opportunity to do?
- If the right opportunity appeared tomorrow, what would you want it to be?

### Skills

- What are your strongest skills?
- Which skills do other people regularly ask you for help with?
- Which skills have you learned through work?
- Which skills have you taught yourself?
- What qualifications or certificates do you have?
- Which skills can you demonstrate through previous work?
- Do you have examples of your work?
- Which skills would you like to improve?
- Which new skills would you like to learn?

### Experience

- What work or projects are you most proud of?
- What organisations or clients have you worked with?
- Have you volunteered?
- Have you led a team or project?
- Have you started something yourself?
- Have you won competitions or awards?
- Have you been featured by media?
- Have you spoken publicly?
- Have you participated in community projects?
- What achievement means the most to you?

### Credibility

- Do you currently have a professional biography?
- Do you have a professional photograph?
- Do you have a CV?
- Do you have a portfolio?
- Do you have references?
- Do you have testimonials?
- Can previous clients or employers verify your work?
- Are your qualifications available for verification?
- Do you have a LinkedIn profile?
- Do you have a website?
- Which social-media platforms represent your professional work?
- What would you like people to immediately understand about you when they discover you online?

### Visibility

- How easy do you think it is for someone to discover you online?
- What do you currently do to promote yourself?
- Have you ever been featured in an article?
- Have you ever been interviewed?
- Would you be comfortable speaking on camera?
- Would you be interested in telling your story through Unplug?
- What topics could you confidently speak about?
- What would you like to become known for?
- Who would you most like to reach?

### Opportunity

- What opportunities are you currently looking for? *(checklist)* Employment · Freelance work · Clients · Collaborations · Speaking opportunities · Modelling opportunities · Sponsorship · Investment · Brand partnerships · Media appearances · Mentorship · Training · Internships · Volunteer experience · Competitions · Networking · International opportunities
- Are you willing to travel for opportunities?
- Are you available for remote opportunities?

### Barriers

- What is currently stopping you from reaching your next level? *(checklist)* Lack of money · Lack of experience · Lack of confidence · Lack of exposure · Lack of contacts/network · Lack of skills · Location · Transportation · Technology or internet access · Lack of information about opportunities
- What support would make the biggest difference to you right now?

### Growth

- Would you like mentorship?
- What would you want mentorship in?
- Would you participate in workshops?
- Would you take short online courses?
- Would you complete development missions?
- Would you participate in challenges that improve your portfolio?
- Would you like Unplug to recommend actions to help you grow?
- How much time could you realistically invest in personal development each week?

### Contribution

- What could you teach someone else?
- Would you mentor another member?
- Would you collaborate with other members?
- Would you volunteer your skills for selected community projects?
- Which causes matter to you?
- What type of people would you like to connect with?

### Personal story

- What challenge have you overcome?
- What has shaped who you are today?
- What accomplishment are you most proud of?
- What lesson have you learned that could help someone else?
- Who inspires you?
- What do you want your legacy to be?
- If Unplug could help the world discover one thing about you, what should it be?

## For businesses

### Business identity

- What is your business name?
- What does your business do?
- What industry are you in?
- Where are you based?
- When did the business start?
- Is the business registered?
- What problem do you solve?
- Who do you solve it for?
- What is your main product or service?
- What makes your business different?

### Founder

- Who founded the business?
- Why was the business started?
- What experience does the founder/team have?
- What is the founder's long-term vision?
- What is the biggest lesson learned since starting?

### Customers

- Who is your ideal customer?
- Are your customers individuals, businesses, government, or organisations?
- Where are your customers located?
- What problem causes customers to seek your solution?
- How do customers currently discover you?
- Why do customers choose you?
- Why do potential customers sometimes choose competitors instead?

### Offering

- What products or services do you offer?
- Which produces the most revenue?
- Which has the highest potential?
- What are your prices?
- How frequently do customers buy?
- Do you offer online purchasing?
- Do you deliver?
- What geographical area can you serve?

### Business stage

- Which best describes you: idea, startup, early revenue, established, growing, or scaling?
- Approximately how many customers do you currently serve?
- How many employees or contractors do you have?
- Is the business generating revenue?
- Is revenue increasing, stable, or declining?
- What is your biggest business expense?
- What is your biggest operational challenge?

### Credibility

- Do you have a registered company?
- Do you have a business bank account?
- Do you have a website?
- Do you have professional email?
- Do you have professional branding?
- Do you have customer reviews?
- Can previous customers verify your work?
- Do you have case studies?
- Do you have a portfolio?
- Do you have certifications or industry accreditation?
- Do you have company policies where required?
- What proof would reassure a customer that your business can deliver?

### Marketing

- How do you currently market the business?
- Which platform generates the most enquiries?
- Approximately how many enquiries do you receive monthly?
- Approximately how many become customers?
- Do you know your customer acquisition cost?
- Do you collect customer information with appropriate consent?
- Do you have an email database?
- Do you have a marketing strategy?
- Do you regularly create content?
- Have you received media coverage?
- What would you like your business to become known for?

### Sales

- How do customers buy from you?
- Do you have a documented sales process?
- What prevents prospects from buying?
- What is your average sale?
- Do you receive repeat business?
- Do you ask customers for referrals?
- What would allow you to double sales?

### Growth

- What is your primary goal for the next 12 months? *(checklist)* More customers · Increased revenue · New locations · More employees · New products · Investment · Equipment · Technology · Exporting · Partnerships · Government contracts · Retail distribution · Franchising
- What would the business look like if everything went well over the next five years?

### Funding

- Are you currently looking for funding?
- How much?
- What would the funding be used for?
- Have you applied previously?
- What happened?
- Do you have financial statements?
- Do you maintain management accounts?
- Do you know your monthly revenue?
- Do you know your gross margin?
- Do you understand your cash flow?
- Would you like assistance becoming funding-ready?

### Market access

- What new market would you most like to enter?
- What type of customer would transform your business?
- Which companies would you like to supply?
- Would you like: *(checklist)* Corporate procurement opportunities · Government opportunities · Export opportunities · Retail opportunities · Online marketplace opportunities
- What prevents you from entering those markets?

### Talent

- Are you currently hiring?
- Which skills do you struggle to find?
- Would you hire: *(checklist)* Freelancers · Interns · Graduates · Creators
- Would you offer workplace experience?
- Would you mentor aspiring entrepreneurs?
- Could your business provide opportunities to Unplug members?

### Partnerships

- What businesses would complement yours?
- What type of partnership are you looking for?
- Would you collaborate on campaigns?
- Would you sponsor events or individuals?
- Would you provide products for competitions?
- Would you participate in community initiatives?
- What could you provide another business?
- What do you need another business to provide you?

### Challenges

- What is your single biggest business challenge?
- What problem keeps appearing?
- What takes too much of your time?
- What expertise are you missing?
- What is preventing the business from growing faster?
- What have you tried already?
- What kind of support would make the biggest difference?

### Unplug

- What would you want Unplug to help you achieve? *(checklist)* More visibility · Credibility · Leads · Customers · Partnerships · Employees · Media coverage · Funding readiness · Training · Mentorship · Networking · Market access
- If Unplug successfully helped your business over the next 12 months, what measurable result would make you say "this worked"?

## Closing questions — both applicant types

- **What do you need right now that nobody seems to be helping you with?**
- **What can you offer that somebody else might currently need?**

These two questions are deliberately separate and prominent. They are the seed of a possible future NEED/OFFER matching engine. The current Growth Application release does **not** implement automatic matching.
